Our Mission

A Section 8 non-profit licensed by the Central Government, committed to creating lasting social impact through grassroots community action across India.

🤝

How We Work

Almost entirely youth-driven, the foundation mobilises volunteers, professionals, and corporate partners to deliver direct, measurable support in underserved communities.

🎯

Our Reach

From food distribution and education to animal rescue and women's empowerment, IAF runs six parallel initiatives addressing the full spectrum of community need.

Our Initiatives

Six Projects. One Mission.

Each initiative targets a distinct dimension of social need — together forming a holistic model of community upliftment.

🍱

Project Seva

Food & Community Welfare

Providing nutritious meals and essential clothing to those in extreme poverty. Through community mapping, Seva delivers dignified, consistent support — not one-time charity.

#ProjectSeva

📚

Project BachpanShala

#ProjectBachpanShala

Social Impact

Why It Matters

Six projects. One unified vision — a more inclusive, compassionate, and empowered India.

InAmigos Foundation addresses the full spectrum of community need simultaneously — from the plate to the classroom, from the streets to the boardroom. Every project targets sustained impact, not one-time relief.

  • 🍱
    Hunger & Basic Dignity — Continuous food and clothing drives for the most vulnerable.
  • 📚
    Education & Future Readiness — Free learning, digital literacy, and mentorship for children.
  • 👩‍💼
    Women's Independence — Skill training and financial literacy that turn dependency into self-reliance.
  • 🐾
    Animal Compassion — Rescue, feeding, and care drives promoting empathy for all living beings.
  • 🌳
    Environmental Stewardship — Plantation and awareness activities for a greener tomorrow.
  • 💼
    Youth Employability — Internships and skill programmes creating India's next generation of change-makers.

Trust & Transparency

Certified. Accountable. Trusted.

Every standard of transparency, quality, and legal compliance — giving donors and partners full confidence.

🏛

Section 8 NGO

Registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 and licensed by the Central Government of India.

📋

80G & 12A Certified

Donors receive full tax exemption benefits under the Income Tax Act — transparent and accountable.

🤝

CSR-1 Registered

Eligible to receive and administer corporate CSR funds directly, enabling impactful company partnerships.

🇮🇳

NITI Aayog / NGO Darpan

Registered on NGO Darpan under NITI Aayog, aligning work with India's national development goals.

⭐

IAF ISO 9001:2015

Holds ISO 9001:2015 certification, signifying the highest standards of quality management in operations.

🌐

Pan-India Operations

Based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, IAF operates across multiple states through dedicated volunteers and professionals.
